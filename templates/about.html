📚 What is Smart Study Planner?

Smart Study Planner is a web-based study management application designed to help students organize their study plans, track completed study hours, monitor progress and view an estimated performance based on their activity.

✨ What You Can Do

📅Plan Your Study

Create study plans with subjects, dates and planned hours.

📈Track Progress

See planned hours, studied hours and overall progress.

🔮View Prediction

Get an estimated performance from your saved study activity.

🎯 Our Goal

The goal of this project is to make study planning easier and give students a clear view of their preparation. Instead of keeping study information in different places, the planner brings it together in one dashboard.
